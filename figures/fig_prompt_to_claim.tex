\caption{\textbf{From one prompt to four claim-level labels.}
The task context $p$ fixes a truth contract with four fields: the archive extract $O_p$ shown top right,
and the three shown in the second band.
The dashed box records the derived summary $\kappa_p$, which condenses the two permission fields into
one ordinal level for cross-resource comparison and is never consulted when a claim is labeled.
The model response is segmented into spans; only spans carrying truth-conditional content yield
canonical claims. Each canonical claim is compared with the archive extract $O_p$, then labeled.
The same response yields \SUP, \SUP$+$\SV, \LD, and \Hall\ under a single contract:
$c_3^*$ is evidence-unknown but lies inside $\Gamma_p$ and is hedged as $\mu_p$ requires, whereas
$c_4^*$ is evidence-unknown, falls outside $\Gamma_p$, and is asserted without the required marking.
The right-hand annotations locate the two stages at which the contract acts:
$\sigma_p$ conditions claim recovery, $O_p$ and the permission fields condition labeling.
The archive extract and the name ``Chapuis'' are constructed for this example.}

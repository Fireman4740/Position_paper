  >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.3cm}X}
\toprule
\textbf{Evaluation setting} & \textbf{Native unit or decision} &
\textbf{Role in this catalogue} \\
\midrule
SimpleQA \citep{wei_measuring_2024} & Short answer graded correct,
incorrect, or not attempted & Supplies a traceable historical question and
gold answer. The catalogue adds a longer response, explicit permission scope,
required marking, and a claim-level distinction between \Hall{} and \LD. \\
FActScore \citep{min_factscore_2023} & Atomic factual precision of long-form
biographical generation relative to a knowledge source & Supplies a traceable
biographical topic and the factuality baseline. The catalogue makes the task
oracle bounded, then contrasts a factual biography with an explicitly licensed
historical reconstruction. \\
Contract-aware proposal & Evidence relation plus $\Gamma$ and $\mu$ for each
recovered claim & Holds $O$, $q^*$, the response span, marking, and style fixed.
An evidence-unknown claim is \Hall{} when out of scope and \LD{} when within
scope and marked as required. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\medskip
\noindent\textit{Scope note.} These ten figures are author-constructed
specification checks, not sampled model outputs and not an empirical estimate
of benchmark coverage.  They show what information a contract-aware extension
would represent beyond the native factuality decisions of the two source
benchmarks.

\medskip
\noindent\textit{Reproducibility note.} SimpleQA row numbers are zero-based
positions in the
\href{https://openaipublic.blob.core.windows.net/simple-evals/simple_qa_test_set.csv}{official evaluation CSV};
FActScore line numbers are one-based positions in the prompt-entity files from
the \href{https://github.com/shmsw25/FActScore}{official repository's data
archive}.  The catalogue paraphrases the SimpleQA questions but preserves their
subjects and gold answers.
